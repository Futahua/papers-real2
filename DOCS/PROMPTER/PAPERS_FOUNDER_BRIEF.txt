================================================================================
1. WHAT PAPERS IS
================================================================================

Papers is a lightweight personal layer over Windows.

Papers persistently identifies, organizes, exposes, enters, tracks, leaves,
and switches Backpacks — one active Backpack at a time.

That is the whole of what Papers universally is. It is deliberately narrow.

Windows, applications, files, folders, projects, services, and outside data
already exist independently beneath Papers. Papers does not own them, does
not absorb them into a proprietary model of reality, and does not claim to
understand or represent the surrounding machine and world beyond what is
needed to do the job above.

================================================================================
2. BACKPACKS
================================================================================

A Backpack is anything the creator manually makes available through Papers as
a distinct place to enter or invoke.

Backpacks may be anything. Two Backpacks need not resemble the same category
of software, share an interface pattern, or share any internal structure.

Backpack internals and construction are unspecified. No universal assumption
may be made about a Backpack's interface, contents, dashboard, notes, files,
chat, history, artifacts, lifecycle, restoration, state model, permission
model, rendering, package format, construction method, resource behavior, AI
integration, safety model, background behavior, or definition of success.

ONE ACTIVE BACKPACK AT A TIME is the only universal runtime assumption Papers
makes about Backpacks.

================================================================================
3. TOOLS
================================================================================

A Tool is a simpler capability that the creator manually chooses to classify
as a Tool rather than as a Backpack. This is an organizational decision made
by the creator, not a technical distinction Papers enforces. Papers does not
maintain a deep ontology separating Tools from Backpacks.

================================================================================
4. AI AND AGENTS
================================================================================

Papers itself does not know or reason about anything.

Agent architecture is unspecified. No global, local, per-Backpack, permanent,
or provider-specific AI arrangement is universal unless the creator
explicitly chooses it for a specific Backpack or purpose. Papers must not be
described as the intelligence itself — any intelligence belongs to agents,
which are a separate concern from what Papers universally is.

================================================================================
5. REUSE-FIRST
================================================================================

Reuse-first is binding law. Before building any new capability:

1. Identify suitable existing products, tools, or systems that already do it.
2. Consider forks, configuration, plugins, composition, and automation over
   the existing landscape before writing new code.
3. Write new code only for the smallest missing Papers responsibility that
   nothing existing already covers.

Do not rebuild inferior internal versions of things that already exist.

================================================================================
6. DESIRED BEHAVIOR BEFORE BUILDS
================================================================================

No implementation work proceeds without a specific desired creator behavior
first. The required sequence is:

  desired creator behavior → existing products and systems →
  reuse investigation → smallest missing Papers responsibility →
  bounded implementation

If desired behavior is unspecified, ask the creator. Do not infer it from the
current implementation, from prior Backpack forms, or from what would be
convenient to build next.

================================================================================
7. CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION IS EVIDENCE, NOT ONTOLOGY
================================================================================

The application code in app/ demonstrates one Backpack form that has been
built and tested. It is evidence that Papers' narrow responsibility (Backpack
identification, organization, entry, tracking, leaving, switching — one
active Backpack at a time) can be implemented and can persist truthfully.

It is not a definition of what all Backpacks must contain. Nothing in the
current implementation — its file references, its notes, its conversation
surface, its activity history, its Desk, its AI engine seam — is universal
Backpack anatomy. Future Backpacks may share none of it.

See DOCS/PAPERS_RECOVERY_MAP_V1.txt for the full classification of what the
current implementation contains and how each piece is classified.

================================================================================
8. WHAT THIS DOCUMENT DOES NOT SAY
================================================================================

This brief does not prescribe, require, or assume:

- a persistent Papers-owned "World" model as product center of gravity;
- a global AI, or any specific AI architecture;
- permanent World-level or cross-Backpack memory;
- Life OS, knowledge management, or any specific Backpack category as a
  required destiny;
- accumulation of durable artifacts as a universal Backpack behavior;
- guarded action, truthfulness mechanics, or provenance as universal Backpack
  identity (they may be sound patterns for Backpacks that choose them, but
  they are not required of every Backpack — a Backpack may deliberately
  operate with different or no such guarantees, at the creator's choice);
- an agent operator/workbench foundation as a required stage;
- that Backpacks overlap, share a world, or relate to each other spatially;
- that "room" or "world" are product ontology (they are historical
  implementation labels only — see DOCS/PAPERS_ONTOLOGY_CLARIFICATION_V1.txt).

Any prior document that claimed the above was drift beyond this definition.
The full history of that drift — what was claimed, by which document, and
what it caused — is preserved in DOCS/PAPERS_RECOVERY_MAP_V1.txt Section C.
That history is not repeated here because this document is meant to be read
without contradiction.
