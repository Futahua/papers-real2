PAPERS_BACKPACK_DESK_V1

The Desk — one Backpack's active work surface. Optional experiment in the
CURRENT IMPLEMENTED Backpack form only. First-form concept document.
Introduced 2026-07-09 as the first active-work experiment inside a Backpack;
shipped in the current implementation and being evaluated through real use.
NOT part of the definition of Backpack. NOT mandatory for any Backpack type.
Future Backpack experiences may use, adapt, replace, or omit the Desk concept
entirely — and the Desk's existence in the current implementation must not be
read as authorization for it to become universal.

Status
- Downstream of PAPERS_FOUNDER_BRIEF_V4 and PAPERS_ONTOLOGY_CLARIFICATION_V1.
- Documents a shipped optional experiment and its deliberate limits. If a
  later document supersedes the Desk's first form, it must say so explicitly.
- RECOVERY CORRECTION (added 2026-07-10): The Desk is an optional experiment
  in the CURRENT Backpack form. The binding creator definition (PAPERS_RECOVERY_MAP_V1.txt)
  prohibits making universal Backpack assumptions — and the Desk is NOT a
  universal Backpack assumption. The self-stated limit in Section 4 of this
  document ("Not a claim that every future Backpack is 'a desk plus notes'")
  is correct and binding.
  KNOWN CODE ISSUE: In the current implementation, getDeskView() is called

AMENDMENT 2026-07-10 — supersedes the first form on one point, explicitly,
per the rule above (the framing correction itself is already made canonically
by the RECOVERY CORRECTION in Status, which governs):

1. IN-PLACE REVISION IS NOT DESK-ONLY. In the current implemented Backpack
   form, in-place artifact revision — first built for the Desk's working
   note — now serves any AI-made note in the Backpack: revised in place
   under the creator's direction, guarded the same way (exact preview
   generated from the same gathering code, explicit approval, durable
   attributable result, honest recorded failure). Each revision records the
   direction used and preserves the replaced title/text in the artifact's
   provenance trail. §4's "one working note per Backpack" limit still
   governs the Desk's own synthesis action; it no longer implies that
   revising in place is Desk-only. Consistent with the recovery correction,
   the assistant's context copy no longer calls the Desk a "default working
   context": when the Desk is in use it is given first attention; the
   assistant stays grounded in the whole Backpack, and an empty Desk leaves
   the Backpack fully inhabitable. This describes the current experiment
   only — it is not a claim about what any future Backpack must offer.

================================================================================
1. WHAT THE DESK IS
================================================================================

A Backpack holds everything that belongs to it: things (references to real
items on the machine), notes, history, conversation. The DESK is the smaller
set of that material which is the Backpack's ACTIVE WORK right now.

The Desk carries three kinds of state, all Papers-owned and durable:

1. THE BRIEF — the creator's current focus for this Backpack, in their own
   words. Distinct from the Backpack description: the description says what
   the Backpack IS; the brief says what the creator is DOING there now.

2. DESK ITEMS — an ordered set of things and notes the creator has placed on
   the Desk. Putting something on the Desk does not move or copy it; taking
   it off does not remove it from the Backpack. Desk membership is work
   state, not storage.

3. THE WORKING NOTE — one durable artifact (kind "working-note") that the AI
   REVISES IN PLACE from the Desk, instead of littering the Backpack with
   one-shot summaries. Same object across revisions; every revision is
   recorded in the artifact's provenance trail (engine, brief used, source
   things with real paths, source notes, timestamp). The artifact-level
   source list always reflects what the CURRENT text was made from.

================================================================================
2. THE ASSISTANT IS DESK-GROUNDED
================================================================================

The Backpack's AI treats the Desk as its default working context. Its
serialized context includes the brief, the desk items (with their live
present/missing status — the Desk never hides missing reality), and the
working note's existence. Ordinary Backpack conversation is therefore
grounded in the active work by default, not in an undifferentiated dump of
everything the Backpack contains.

The Desk's native action is SYNTHESIZE: turn the brief + desk things (read
truthfully, with capped content and honest truncation markers) + desk notes
+ the previous working note into a revised working note. It is guarded the
same way as every Papers AI action: an exact preview of precisely what will
be shared, then explicit approval, then a durable, attributable result.
Failures are honest and recorded in Backpack history; nothing is ever faked.

================================================================================
3. TRUTHFULNESS RULES
================================================================================

- The Desk may only reference objects that actually exist in the Backpack;
  detaching a thing from the Backpack takes it off the Desk as part of the
  same recorded event.
- Desk things are re-verified against reality like all thing references;
  a missing thing is shown missing on the Desk itself.
- Desk changes are Backpack events (desk-added, desk-removed,
  brief-updated, note-updated) carrying refs, so the Backpack's timeline
  remains a truthful, navigable record of the work.
- The synthesis preview and the executed action are generated from the same
  gathering code, so the preview is exact by construction.

================================================================================
4. WHAT THE DESK IS NOT (first-form limits, stated deliberately)
================================================================================

This is the FIRST form of Backpack inhabitation, not the final shape of
Backpacks:

- Not a task manager: no checkboxes, statuses, assignees, or deadlines.
- Not a claim that every future Backpack is "a desk plus notes". Future
  Backpack kinds may have entirely different active-work forms; the Desk is
  the proving slice for "a Backpack knows what is being worked on".
- Not an autonomous agent surface: the AI acts on the Desk only through the
  guarded, creator-initiated synthesis action and Desk-grounded replies.
- One working note per Backpack for now. Multiple parallel work streams per
  Backpack are a plausible later step, not silently pre-built.

Persistence: rooms/<id>/desk.json ({brief, briefUpdatedAt, items[],
workingNoteId}). Pre-Desk Backpacks simply have no desk.json and read as an
empty Desk — no migration needed.

End of PAPERS_BACKPACK_DESK_V1.
